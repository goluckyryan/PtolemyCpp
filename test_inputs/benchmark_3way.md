## Timing Benchmark (2026-06-27)

Single-threaded, sequential, identical inputs. 40 representative cases.
Host: spark-c94b (NVIDIA DGX Spark, aarch64). Cleopatra runs under qemu-i386.

| Code | Mean/run | Total (40) | Relative |
|------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Cleopatra (Fortran, qemu-i386) | 2.357 s | 94.27 s | baseline |
| Ptolemy-f2c (native aarch64)   | 0.393 s | 15.70 s | 6.0x faster |
| PtolemyCpp (native aarch64)    | 0.102 s |  4.10 s | 23.0x faster |

**Speedups (wall-clock per run):**
- PtolemyCpp vs Cleopatra(qemu): **23.0x** faster
- PtolemyCpp vs f2c            : **3.83x** faster
- f2c        vs Cleopatra(qemu): 6.0x faster

Notes:
- The Cleopatra figure is inflated by qemu-i386 EMULATION (32-bit x86 binary on
  ARM). On native x86 it would be much closer to f2c. The 23x is partly an
  artifact of running emulated on this aarch64 host.
- The **f2c -> PtolemyCpp 3.83x is the honest like-for-like number**: same arch,
  same compiler. The clean C++17 rewrite is ~4x faster than the mechanical
  f2c translation.
- Run time scales with target mass / lmax (more partial waves). Heaviest cases:
  Cleopatra/qemu ~4.8s, PtolemyCpp stays under ~0.17s.
